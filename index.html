About My Work

Throughout my time at Indiana Universtiy, I developed a strong foundation in digital art and frontend development. I have worked along side professors and classmates to create full data systems to interactive digital art. My work focuses on visual storytelling, experimentation with digital spaces, and a specialty in frontend Web Design and System Development with design in focus. I have a strong interest in creating interactive experiences that are both visually engaging and user-friendly. My goal is to continue to push the boundaries of digital art and web development, creating innovative projects that inspire and engage with users.
