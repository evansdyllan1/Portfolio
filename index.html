About Me

Hi! I am Dyllan Evans, and I recently graduated with a Bachelor's in Informatics with a focus in Digital Art and Design, along with a minor in Web Design. I made this portfolio website from scratch as a part of my personal projects to showcase my work in digital art, creative coding, and full-stack web development. I have always had an interest in web development, videography and editing, alongside digital and physical 2D/3D art and illustration. Please feel free to reach out through the buttons above. Enjoy exploring my work and website!

About My Work

I focus in front-end development with a focus on user interaction and experience, I enjoy curating a website that puts the user in mind. This translates to my digital artwork, as I focus on what I want the user to think and feel while experiencing it. Throughout my projects, you will see a continuation of nostalgia with practical design. This is seen in my front-end development through including fun aspects, colors, and design that intrigue the user. As I further my career, I want to continue combining front-end development and digital art to create work that brings creativity into practical technology.
